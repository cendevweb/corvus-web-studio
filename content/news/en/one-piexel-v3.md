The Corvus Direct walks through v3, in order and controller in hand. No slides, no empty promises: everything shown is already live.

### On the programme

1. **Ranked matchmaking and its bounties.** Two queues, nine tiers and a WANTED poster: the bounty climbs from Cabin Boy to Pirate King.
2. **The roster's newcomers.** The twelve fighters that arrived in October, with parry, super armor, slow motion and guard break.
3. **What changes under the hood.** The combat engine and online play: the game predicts the opponent's inputs, then rolls back up to 8 frames to replay.

> One Piexel is played in the browser, with nothing to install: keyboard, touch or gamepad.

For the full list of changes, the [October 18](/en/news/one-piexel-patch-2026-10-18) and [October 19](/en/news/one-piexel-patch-2026-10-19) patch notes remain the reference.
