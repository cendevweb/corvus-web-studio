Warcraft and Brands join Fastr's catalogue.

- **2** new dictionaries: Warcraft and Brands.
- **19** themes in total, from Animals to Star Wars.
- **175,000+** words in the dictionaries.

Every dictionary goes through the same pipeline: source collection, aliases, validation, then behaviour tests per theme. Matching still tolerates accents, aliases and small typos: one typo per word, two from 12 letters up.

The rule itself does not move: one theme, 4 rounds of 120 seconds, and the fastest wins.
