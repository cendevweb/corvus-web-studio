Fastr's V2 redesign is live. New interface, new screens, new guide: from the home screen to the results, every step of a game has been redrawn, and the game now explains itself to newcomers.

## In short | A game redrawn from start to finish | In short

- **5** game presets, from Classic to Custom
- **22** themes to search and filter by category
- **40** items explained in the codex: bonuses, penalties and trophies, it's all there

## Home and lobby | One way in, a brand-new lobby | Lobby

The home screen fits on one page: your nickname, your badge, then "New game" or a four-letter code to join friends. Your nickname and badge are kept on your device, so there is no need to type them again.

The lobby has been rebuilt from scratch. At a glance you see the theme and its word count, a summary of the rules, and everything you need to invite people: a short code, a QR code and a button to copy the link. The player grid holds up to 100 players, and a countdown starts the game automatically if the host has not started it first.

Bots are added from the lobby and now carry real-looking nicknames (LoupFéroce, MmeCassoulet, samuel2008…): in game, you can no longer tell them apart from players.

## Invitation | Join in one click | Invitation

An invitation link opens a page that tells you everything before you join: who is inviting you, the theme, the number of players and the rules. All that is left is to pick a nickname and join the lobby.

## Themes | Pick your theme | Themes

Choosing the theme now happens in a new window: a search bar, category filters (Movies & TV, Video games, Manga, General knowledge) and the word count of each theme. No idea? The "Surprise theme" button picks one for you. The theme applies to the whole lobby, and players see it change live.

## Rules | Games made to measure | Rules

The host can now set up the game their way, starting from five presets:

- **Classic.** 4 rounds of 2 minutes, 20 words to finish.
- **Sprint.** 3 one-minute rounds, 10 words to finish.
- **Chaos.** A penalty on every word.
- **Purist.** No bonuses, no penalties.
- **Custom.** Your own settings.

Everything can be adjusted: the number of rounds, their length (1 to 3 minutes), the number of words to finish (10, 15, 20 or 30), a fixed theme or a random one each round, how often bonuses and penalties trigger, and even the list of effects in play. By default, a penalty flies every 2 words found, and a bonus drops every 3 correct answers in a row.

## Codex | Every effect explained | Codex

The "Rules" button opens a codex with four tabs: the game, bonuses, penalties and trophies. Each effect has its own card, with what it does and how long it lasts. A few examples:

```cards
bolt | Combo boost | +50 points per word found for 6 seconds.
shield | Mental shield | The next penalty you receive is blocked.
keyboard | Forced letter | For 15 seconds, your words must start with a given letter.
break | Scramble | For 7 seconds, the letters you type are displayed out of order.
```

Trophies are handed out at the end of the game. Some are earned, like Winner or Accurate; others are endured, like Tortoise or Chocolate.

## New players | The game explains itself | Guide

Newcomers no longer need someone to walk them through the rules. In the lobby, an animated demo shows the essentials in a few steps, and can be skipped in one click. In the first round, cues guide your typing, and when a word is rejected, the reason stays on screen for 3 seconds so you have time to read it.

These aids can be turned off in the settings, with the "Beginner help" option.

## Recaps | Podium and detailed results | Recaps

Between rounds, a new screen shows the podium, your round recap and the overall standings. At the end of the game, the results break down your place round by round, your bonuses and penalties, and the trophies won.

## Settings | Tuned for you | Settings

A new settings screen brings together sound (music, sound effects, music lowered during rounds, shuffle), your identity, and three comfort options:

- **Beginner help.** The lobby demo and the first-round cues.
- **Reduce animations.** Less shaking, softer transitions.
- **Toned-down penalty effects.** No flashes or static, for photosensitive players. A toned-down penalty keeps its rule and its duration.
