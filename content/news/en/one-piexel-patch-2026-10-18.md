Kaido, Katakuri, Garp, Arlong and eight others join the roster. They arrive with four brand-new mechanics, and on the same day, ranked matchmaking opens its queues.

## In short

The biggest patch since release.

- **12** new fighters, for a roster of 51 pirates, marines and warlords.
- **4** new mechanics: parry, super armor, slow motion, guard break.
- **9** ranked tiers, from Cabin Boy to Pirate King, with a bounty in berries.
- **3** ways to play: keyboard, touch and gamepad, on desktop and phone alike.

## The 12 new fighters

Twelve at once. Emperors, marines, Baroque Works agents and a few pirates everyone loves to hate. Each one comes with light and heavy attacks, directional specials, a grab, an ultimate and a max ultimate.

Arlong, Foxy, Garp, Kaido (1250 HP), Kaku, Katakuri, Killer, Kuro (880 HP), Morgan, Mr. 1, Mr. 2, Tashigi.

The two extremes of the batch: Kuro, the most fragile with 880 HP, and Kaido, the sturdiest with 1250 HP.

Between the two, the full range. Kaido soaks up damage and moves forward. Kuro strikes first and must never get hit: with him, the slightest mistake is costly.

![A One Piexel fight on the deck of the Baratie, special attack in progress](/images/games/one-piexel/op-52.jpg "A 1-on-1 fight, best of 3 rounds.")

## Four new mechanics

The newcomers bring more than sprites. Four mechanics enter the engine with them, and they change how an exchange is approached.

- **Parry.** A well-timed counter that turns the exchange around.
- **Super armor.** Some moves absorb hits without being interrupted: the damage goes through, but the attack comes out.
- **Slow motion.** Time drags for a moment for whoever suffers it. Enough to open a window.
- **Guard break.** Available at half meter, to punish an opponent hiding behind their guard.

## Ranked matchmaking: a bounty to raise

Two queues from now on: casual to play without pressure, ranked to put a price on your head. Rank is a bounty in berries, displayed on a WANTED poster.

9 tiers. The bounty can climb up to 10 billion berries, and number 1 holds the JOY BOY title.

- 5 placement matches before getting a first bounty.
- A tier shield, to avoid dropping at the first loss.
- A search that widens if the wait gets long, rather than leaving a player alone in the queue.
- 3 ranked matches at most per 24 h against the same player, to keep the leaderboard honest.

No need to stare at the screen while waiting: during the search, arcade and training stay playable. The fight starts as soon as an opponent is found.

## Mobile and gamepad

One Piexel is played in the browser, with nothing to install. With this patch, three ways to play: keyboard, touch, gamepad.
