The day after the twelve-fighter patch, three adjustments: a throw that swaps corners, a Leaderboard screen available from the queue, and a dozen rebalanced max ultimates.

## Combat | A back throw

A new throw joins the grab: it **swaps corners**. A player pinned against the edge of the screen can now trade places with their opponent instead of taking it.

## Online | The top 10, from the queue | The top 10

The Leaderboard screen can be opened while searching for an opponent. It shows the top 10 biggest bounties of the moment, and the JOY BOY title of number 1.

![One Piexel: the Leaderboard screen, top 10 biggest bounties of the moment](/images/games/one-piexel/classement.jpg "The Leaderboard screen: the ten biggest bounties of the moment.")

## Balancing | Max ultimates that matter, even on guard | Max ultimates

A dozen max ultimates did next to nothing against a guarding opponent. They finally deal part of their damage.

| Max ultimate | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Against a guarding opponent | almost no damage | up to 60% of the damage |
