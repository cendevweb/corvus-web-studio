It was announced, and it has been live since November 21: Iconic gets its eighth set, a new way to unlock sets, and the Mission Board. Three features designed together: you play, you earn ICoins, and those ICoins open the next sets.

## In short | Three features that go together | In short

- **50** figures to guess in set 8
- **3,000** ICoins to unlock Historical Figures
- **3** sets that stay free

## Set 8 | Historical Figures | Set 8

Set 8 brings together 50 figures who left their mark on their era. As always, each one is summed up by an object on an illustrated card: an aerial screw, a telescope, an apple tree, a space suit… and you have three tries to find the figure's name.

Made a mistake? The card turns coral: switch to another icon and try again.

## ICoins | Sets unlock as you play | Sets for ICoins

Sets are now unlocked in the shop with ICoins, the in-game currency. Historical Figures costs **3,000 ICoins**: open the set's page and unlock it.

You earn those ICoins by playing:

- **Every icon found.** 25 to 50 ICoins per correct answer.
- **The streak.** Chaining correct answers multiplies your earnings, up to ×2.
- **Missions.** Every completed challenge pays out ICoins (see below).

To go faster, the shop also offers packs of 500, 1,000 or 2,000 ICoins. And nothing changes for the **Video games**, **Shonen** and **Sports** sets: they stay free.

## New | The Mission Board | Missions

This is the big feature of the update. The Mission Board opens from the game screen and lists challenges to take on, each with its progress bar and its ICoins reward. A counter shows the rewards ready to be claimed.

It has two parts:

- **Special missions.** They change with the current set. For Historical Figures: find great conquerors, crowned heads, scientific geniuses…
- **Ongoing objectives.** Long-term challenges tied to your collection: gather 50 characters, find a character in three different sets, pair a hero with their nemesis…

Missions go up in tiers: once you have found 4 crowned heads, the mission moves up to 8, with a bigger reward at the end. And on top of ICoins, missions earn you badges.

## In a hurry | Changelog

- **SHOP** Packs of 500, 1,000 and 2,000 ICoins.
- **ICOINS** 25 to 50 ICoins per icon found, earnings multiplied up to ×2 by the streak.
- **FREE** The Video games, Shonen and Sports sets stay free.
